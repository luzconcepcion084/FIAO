Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

You can sign up by clicking 'Sign Up' on the login screen, choosing your role as Business Owner or Customer, and filling in your credentials.

What's the difference between Business Owner and Customer?

Business Owners manage store inventory, customer credit limits, and financial reports. Customers can view their active debts, payments, and linked stores.

How do I register a new credit?

Go to the Credits section, click '+ New Credit', select the customer, enter the product description, quantity, price, and due date, then save.

How can I change a customer's credit limit?

Navigate to Clients, select 'View Details' on the customer card, and click 'Edit' to modify their credit limit or auto-blocking rules.

How do partial payments work?

In Credits, click 'Register Payment' on any active debt. You can enter an amount lower than the total balance. The system will update the remaining debt automatically.

How do I link my account to a business?

Customers can request to link their account using the store business code. Once the owner approves the request, it will appear in their dashboard.

How can I add products to my inventory?

Go to Products, click '+ Add Product', fill in the name, category, price, stock, and upload or generate an image if desired.

Can I export reports?

Yes! In the Reports section, you can use the 'Export PDF' and 'Export Excel' buttons to download real formatted files based on your filters.

How can I recover my password?

Click 'Forgot Password' on the login screen and follow the instructions sent to your registered email address.

How do payment reminders work?

When a credit is overdue, a reminder button appears. Clicking it lets you customize a professional message and open WhatsApp or your email app to send it manually.

What happens if a customer doesn't pay?

The system tracks overdue dates and blocks new credit requests if auto-blocking is enabled, helping you manage store financial risk safely.
